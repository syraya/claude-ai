Common questions

OMO isn't blocking my apps

Check that OMO has Screen Time access: in OMO, go to Settings › Permissions, or in your iPhone's Settings, go to Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access. Then make sure your block list actually has apps in it (Block Lists tab).

Blocking only works on a real iPhone, not in a simulator.

My schedule didn't turn on

Schedules run even when OMO is closed, but iOS can be a minute or so late. Check the schedule is switched on, that today is one of its days, and that its block list isn't empty. Opening OMO during the block always turns it on.

A friend holds my key and their phone died

In the Circle tab, use the emergency exit. You can unlock yourself after a 60-second wait, and your keyholder is told you used it.

Why do I have to wait before a break?

That pause is the point: a few seconds to ask if you really want the break. You can change how long it is in Settings › Breaks.

How do I unlock more OMOs?

Most come with lifetime focus hours. Others come from adding friends, finishing sessions, keeping streaks, Study Circles, or focusing 25 minutes on certain holidays. Tap a locked OMO to see what it takes.

How do I delete my account?

In OMO, go to Settings › Delete account. That removes your profile, friends and shared stats from our servers. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
