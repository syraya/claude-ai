Schedules that block 9 to 5 or at bedtime

Study Circles with a 60-second emergency exit

Start a Study Circle and every phone in it locks. Each person's key is held by a friend in the circle.

  • KeyholderOnly your keyholder can let you out early: they allow a break, deny it or end your lock.
  • If their phone diesThe emergency exit opens after 60 seconds, and your keyholder is told.
  • No deleting your way outWhile a friend holds your key, iOS won't let you delete apps, OMO included.

33 OMOs to earn

Each OMO is a figure from myth with its own colours and breathing scene. You earn them with focus hours, streaks, friends, Study Circles and 25 minutes of focus on certain holidays.

Questions and support. Email us and we reply within a couple of days.

OMO isn't blocking my apps

Check OMO has Screen Time access in Settings, then Screen Time, then Apps with Screen Time Access. Blocking works on a real iPhone only.

My schedule didn't block my apps

iOS can start a schedule a minute late. Check it is switched on and today is one of its days. Opening OMO during the block turns it on.

Leaving a circle after 60 seconds

In the Circle tab, use the emergency exit. You can leave after a 60-second wait, and your keyholder is told.

The 15-second wait before a break

The pause is the point: a few seconds to ask if you want the break. Change its length in Settings, then Breaks.

Earning all 33 OMOs

Most come with focus hours; others with friends, sessions, streaks, Study Circles and holidays. A locked OMO shows how close you are.

Privacy and deleting your account

In OMO, open Settings and choose Delete account. Your profile, friends and stats are removed from our servers.
