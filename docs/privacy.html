OMO Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

OMO helps you block distracting apps and study with friends. Here's what it keeps, where, and why.

On your phone only

Your block lists, schedules, focus sessions, settings, OMOs and lifetime totals stay on your phone. Apple gives OMO anonymous tokens for the apps you pick, so not even OMO can see their names.

In the cloud, only if you sign in

To make friends and Study Circles work, OMO stores these in Google Firebase: your first name (from Sign in with Apple, and you can change it), a random friend code, your friends list, the study sessions you join, and daily totals: focus minutes, sessions, breaks, and roughly how long you spent in the apps you choose to share. Only you and the friends you've accepted can see your stats.

Notifications

If you sign in, OMO stores a device token so it can tell you when a friend unlocks you or asks you for a break.

What OMO never does

No ads, no selling data, and no tracking you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Settings › Delete account in OMO removes everything stored in the cloud. Deleting the app removes everything on your phone.
